Small pill that labels a status or a player level.

## Status badges

Uppercase `caption` text, 6 px by 12 px padding, `radius-pill`. Each status uses a text and fill pair and always carries a word:

- Open: `primary-tint-text` on `primary-tint`
- Full: `text` on `disabled-bg`
- Confirmed: `success` on `success-bg`
- Almost full: `warning` on `warning-bg`
- Cancelled: `danger` on `danger-bg`

## Level badges

Use `numeric-m` numerals in a pill with 3 px by 14 px padding. Fill by band: Starter `disabled-bg`, Improver `primary-tint`, Mid `primary` with `on-primary`, Advanced `surface-inverse` with `surface-inverse-text`, Pro `accent` with `on-accent`. The 0–7 scale is a sample and configurable; show the number, not only the color.
